###### Appfigures pricing FAQ

### \#\#\#\#\#\# What does Appfigures pricing look like?

Appfigures pricing starts at $7.99/mo for the Connect plan; a free Starter plan is available; paid plans go up to $599.99/mo. Billed monthly or annually.

### \#\#\#\#\#\# How much does Appfigures cost?

Appfigures's cheapest business-usable plan is Connect at $9.99/mo. Provides core universal analytics and revenue tracking suitable for small businesses.

### \#\#\#\#\#\# Does Appfigures have a free tier?

Yes — Appfigures publishes a free tier. Starter plan with limited features.

### \#\#\#\#\#\# Can I buy Appfigures without talking to sales?

Yes — Appfigures's purchase motion is self-serve. A checkout URL is published on the pricing page. No demo is required.
